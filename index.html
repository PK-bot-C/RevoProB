Sensing

The controller runs firmware 1.0. Flash RevoPro_FW_v1.1.0 to use these settings and breathing detection.
The sensors sit on the pump line, so the controller only trusts a reading once pump and valve have been off this long. Raise it if the bags keep creeping after a fill.

Calibration and faults

The controller vents and zeroes the pressure sensors every time it powers on. To redo it by hand: stand up, deflate all, wait until the bags are flat, then tap Tare.
